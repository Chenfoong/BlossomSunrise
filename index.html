About Sunrise Education 🌻

阳光学府 – A story of heart, hope, and home-based learning.

Sunrise Education was founded with a simple belief: every child deserves to be seen, heard, and nurtured. We are not a factory-model school. We are a close-knit learning community in Klang, Selangor, where students aged 7 to 18 thrive through the Cambridge IGCSE curriculum in a homeschool setting.

Our approach blends academic rigour with genuine care. Small cohorts, dedicated mentors, and a curriculum that adapts to the child—not the other way around. We prioritise emotional wellbeing, curiosity, and the joy of learning above test scores alone.

At Sunrise, we don't just prepare students for exams. We prepare them for life—with confidence, compassion, and a lifelong love for learning.

Cambridge IGCSE at Sunrise

The world's most trusted international qualification—delivered with heart.

The Cambridge IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) is a globally respected qualification for students typically aged 14–18. At Sunrise Education, we deliver the IGCSE curriculum in a supportive homeschool environment, preparing students for top universities and fulfilling careers.

1🌐

Globally Recognised

Accepted by universities and employers in over 160 countries. A truly international passport to higher education.

2📋

Broad Subject Choice

Over 70 subjects available. Students can specialise in their strengths while maintaining a well-rounded profile.

3🧠

Critical Thinking Focus

IGCSE emphasises analysis, evaluation, and problem-solving—not just memorisation. Skills for life.

4🎓

Perfect A-Level Bridge

Seamless progression to Cambridge AS & A-Levels, IB Diploma, or foundation programmes worldwide.

5📝

Fair Assessment Methods

Mix of written exams, practicals, and coursework. Different learners get different ways to shine.

6🔍

Independent Learning

IGCSE builds self-discipline and research skills—essential for university and beyond.

7📊

Benchmarked Standards

Rigorous, consistent grading across the globe. An IGCSE grade means the same in Malaysia as in Manchester.

8🏛️

University Ready

Top universities—including Oxford, Cambridge, Harvard, and NUS—recognise and value IGCSE qualifications.

9📖

Deep Subject Knowledge

Each subject is explored in meaningful depth, building genuine expertise and intellectual curiosity.

10🏡

Homeschool-Friendly

IGCSE is highly adaptable to homeschool settings. Flexible exam scheduling suits individual readiness.
